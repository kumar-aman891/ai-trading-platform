# Research Skill

Given a symbol or investment question:
1. Resolve instrument identity.
2. Fetch canonical price/volume data.
3. Fetch secondary context only when needed.
4. Compute requested technicals.
5. Gather current material news/events.
6. Gather fundamentals and valuation context.
7. Identify regime and major risks.
8. Produce a structured thesis with evidence timestamps.

Do not issue an executable live order as the output of this skill.
